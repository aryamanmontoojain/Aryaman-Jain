About

About Me

I'm an electronics and communication engineer who enjoys turning timing diagrams into working hardware. I completed my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at Nirma University (minor in Robotics & Automation) and began my MS in Electrical Engineering at San José State University.

My research internships at ISRO and the Physical Research Laboratory gave me hands-on experience with SoC FPGAs, static timing analysis, fixed-point DSP pipelines and on-board debugging.

    Research Intern, FPGA & DSP

    Physical Research Laboratory (PRL)

    • Designed a 4-channel, 32-bit digital lock-in amplifier on the Artix-7 XC7A100T with a 24-bit ADS1278 ADC, DDS/NCO reference, I/Q mixing and multi-rate filtering.

    • Reached 228.519 MHz worst-case and 329.489 MHz best-case against a 200 MHz target.
    • Debugged AXI PS-PL communication using Python, JTAG, OpenOCD and GDB.
